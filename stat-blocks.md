## Locke — Rakshasa (Modified for Level 6 Party)

*Medium fiend, lawful evil*

The standard Rakshasa (CR 13) would TPK a level 6 party. This version is **weakened** — Locke has spent significant power maintaining his disguise and preparing the ritual. His Limited Magic Immunity is reduced, and his HP is lower.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 16 (natural armor) |
| **HP** | 110 (reduced from 150 — the ritual is draining him) |
| **Speed** | 40 ft. |
| **STR** | 14 (+2) |
| **DEX** | 17 (+3) |
| **CON** | 18 (+4) |
| **INT** | 16 (+3) |
| **WIS** | 16 (+3) |
| **CHA** | 20 (+5) |

**Skills:** Deception +10, Insight +8, Perception +8
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** Piercing from magic weapons wielded by good creatures
**Damage Immunities:** Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 18
**Languages:** Common, Infernal

### Weakened Magic Immunity
Locke is **immune to spells of 3rd level or lower** (reduced from 6th). Spells of 4th level or higher affect him normally. He can still choose to be affected by a spell if he wishes.

> **IMPORTANT — This party has no spell slots above 3rd level.** At the start of the fight, Locke is immune to every spell the PCs can cast. See the **Dynamic Brazier Mechanic** below and in the DM guide (Act 6) for how this changes mid-combat.

### Dynamic Brazier Interaction
Each extinguished brazier reduces Locke's spell immunity by one level:
- **5 lit (start):** Immune to 3rd level and below
- **4 lit (1 out):** Immune to 2nd level and below — *3rd-level spells now work (Counterspell, Fireball, Dispel Magic, Spirit Guardians)*
- **3 lit (2 out):** Immune to 1st level and below — *2nd-level spells now work (Hold Person, Suggestion, Heat Metal)*
- **2 lit (3 out):** Immune to cantrips only — *1st-level spells now work. Ritual fails. Locke loses concentration.*
- **1 or 0 lit:** **No spell immunity at all** — *cantrips work. Full arsenal available.*

### Innate Spellcasting
**Spellcasting ability:** Charisma (spell save DC **15**, +7 to hit with spell attacks) *(weakened from DC 18/+10 — the ritual is draining his power)*

- **At will:** Detect Thoughts, Disguise Self, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion
- **3/day each:** Charm Person, Detect Magic, Suggestion
- **1/day each:** Dominate Person, Fly, Plane Shift (self only — escape option if GM wants)

### Multiattack
Locke makes two claw attacks.

### Claw
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) slashing damage, and the target is cursed if it is a creature. The curse takes effect whenever Locke targets the cursed creature with an attack or a harmful spell — Locke has advantage on attack rolls against the cursed creature.

### Tactics
1. **Round 1:** Cast **Dominate Person** on **Jean LeMarque** — the strongest martial PC with the weakest WIS save (WIS DC 15; Jean saves at +2, needs 13+, 60% chance of failure). **Smart move:** Command Jean to move away from the party — his Aura of Protection (+3 to saves) leaves with him, making the remaining PCs vulnerable to the ritual and Locke's other spells.
2. **Round 2-3:** If Dominate worked, command Jean to attack allies (he deals 30-40 damage/round with Extra Attack + Smite). Use **Suggestion** on a second PC (*"You should leave — this isn't your fight"*) — note: Suggestion is a 2nd-level spell, so it's blocked by spell immunity unless 2+ braziers are out. Fall back to claw attacks on whoever is disrupting braziers.
3. **Half HP:** Drops spellcasting, goes full melee. Two claw attacks per turn. Targets whoever damaged him most. Snarls: *"Enough! I'll peel the souls from your bodies the old-fashioned way."*
4. **Key threat:** If Martin casts Dispel Magic on Jean to break Dominate Person (targets Jean, not Locke — works despite immunity), Locke should prioritize clawing Martin to disrupt future Dispels.
5. **Dying monologue:** *"You think this ends with me? The box will be opened. If not tonight... soon."*

### Important for Level 6 Balance
- Locke's nonmagical damage immunity is the biggest challenge. This party's magic damage sources:
  - **Oda's Primal Strike** (beast form attacks count as magical — most reliable from Round 1)
  - **Jean's Magic Weapon** (2nd-level spell on his own weapon — cast on the weapon, not Locke, so immunity doesn't interfere)
  - **Jean's Divine Smite** (radiant damage via weapon attack, not a spell — always works, but the weapon's base B/P/S damage is still blocked without Magic Weapon)
  - **The +1 ornate dagger** in the warehouse — place it visibly on a crate for the Rogue (3 PCs have passive Perception 16)
- If the party has no magic weapons active: after the ritual is disrupted (3 braziers out), Locke's nonmagical immunity downgrades to **resistance** as his power wanes. This is the safety valve.
- The Cult Fanatics provide action economy. Don't add more enemies — this fight is already tough.
- **Aura of Protection (+3 saves)** from Jean makes the ritual saves much easier. The real threat is Locke dominating Jean and moving the Aura away from the party.
- **Path to the Grave + Smite combo** can deal ~32 damage in one attack (vulnerability doubles everything). Expect it. It's dramatic and should be rewarded.
- **Lómë's Sentinel at Death's Door** negates critical hits (3/LR) — great defense against Locke's claws or a dominated Jean.

## Cult Fanatic (x2)

*Medium humanoid, any alignment*

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| **AC** | 13 (leather armor) |
| **HP** | 33 (6d8 + 6) |
| **Speed** | 30 ft. |
| **STR** | 11 (+0) |
| **DEX** | 14 (+2) |
| **CON** | 12 (+1) |
| **INT** | 10 (+0) |
| **WIS** | 13 (+1) |
| **CHA** | 14 (+2) |

**Skills:** Deception +4, Persuasion +4, Religion +2
**Senses:** Passive Perception 11

### Spellcasting
5th-level spellcaster. Spell save DC 11, +3 to hit.

- **Cantrips:** Light, Sacred Flame (2d8), Thaumaturgy
- **1st level (4 slots):** Command, Inflict Wounds, Shield of Faith
- **2nd level (3 slots):** Hold Person, Spiritual Weapon
- **3rd level (2 slots):** Spirit Guardians

### Multiattack
One melee attack and one Sacred Flame.

### Dagger
*Melee/Ranged:* +4 to hit, 1d4 + 2 piercing.

### Tactics
- One casts **Spirit Guardians** and stands near the circle to punish PCs who try to extinguish braziers.
- The other opens with **Hold Person** on a PC who escaped the circle, then uses **Spiritual Weapon** + **Sacred Flame** each round.
- They fight to the death — they're true believers.
